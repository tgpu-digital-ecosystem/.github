## About TGPU

**TGPU Digital Ecosystem** brings together practical digital solutions across **education, language learning, community support, micro-business productivity, and cross-border business connectivity**.

Our approach is simple: build for real needs, prioritise mobile accessibility, support multilingual audiences where useful, and improve products through testing and feedback.

The ecosystem includes independent products at different stages of development. Not every listed product is a finished or commercially available service.

## Development principles

- **Practical first:** Solve a focused user problem before adding complexity.
- **Mobile-friendly:** Design for accessibility, responsive layouts, and everyday devices.
- **Multilingual where useful:** Support Bahasa Melayu, English, and Arabic according to product needs.
- **Privacy and security:** Respect user data; separate internal operations and commercial information from public-facing resources.
- **Reliable delivery:** Use version control, review, QA, and deployment checks appropriate to each product.
- **Transparent status:** Distinguish released capabilities, beta work, planned features, and third-party approvals.
